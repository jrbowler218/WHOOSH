About Whoosh

Whoosh finds discounted clothing from UK retailers and puts the best deals first, so you don't have to check every sale page yourself.

How we rank deals

Each item gets a score out of 100: discount size (35%), popularity (30%), how recently the price dropped (15%), customer rating (10%) and stock (10%). If a retailer doesn't share a signal, we leave it out of that item's score instead of counting it as zero, and mark the item "Limited data".

How Whoosh makes money

When you click through and buy, the retailer may pay us a commission. It costs you nothing extra and doesn't change an item's rank. See our affiliate disclosure.

What we don't do

We don't sell clothes ourselves. Purchases, delivery and returns are handled by the retailer.

Whoosh may earn a commission if you buy through links on this site, at no extra cost to you. Prices and stock can change, so check the retailer's page before you buy.
